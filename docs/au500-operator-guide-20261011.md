yourself and sign in with the existing Outreach Inbox Basic Auth credentials.
This release was verified through metadata; Codex did not open the live panel.

1. Filter Australia and NEEDS_RESEARCH. The five approved prospects are now
   present. The remaining 495 stay in Google Sheets and are not Inbox records.
2. Open a lead. Record verified business activity, identity, findings and useful
   opportunities with public source URLs, dates and factual notes. Source claims
   are provenance until supported; a missing website field proves no absence.
3. Verify the exact contact route when evidence supports it. Australian contact
   details alone do not establish permission; complete the required review.
4. Write and save a working email or exact social DM. Keep the frozen signature
   and opt-out text. Saved copy does not approve or send anything.
5. Use Save & Re-evaluate. Follow the named evidence blockers and next actions.
   Request review and approve only the exact checked content when eligible.
6. For approved email, use the restricted panel's manual copy workflow, review
   the recipient/content yourself, and send only through your own deliberate
   mailbox action. Automated Zoho draft creation remains unavailable. MARK_SENT
   requires actual sending and the panel's confirmation gates.

Record replies and follow-up decisions through the lead controls so earlier
history remains available. Never change a status label to bypass evidence or
suppression. Leave the five Phase 2 engines inactive/manual and the 500 source
research holds intact. Further bulk intake or research needs its own bounded
authorization.

One intentional page load currently uses one Cloud execution and eight Google
GETs. Saves use their normal audited execution/read/write path. Avoid incidental
reloads. The completed validation ceiling is separate from subscription capacity
and intentional business use. Instance storage and managed Google quotas remain
unknown under the accepted pilot exceptions; stop on storage/quota errors.
