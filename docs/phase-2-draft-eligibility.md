# Draft evidence and outreach permission

Research eligibility, draft eligibility and send eligibility are separate decisions.
Phase 1 research selection and qualification stay unchanged. Phase 2 consumes stored
Arc Research read-only and owns the review queue and its operator projection.

A draft needs sufficiently verified identity, meaningful verified business evidence
and a truthful web opportunity. Historical contact, suppression, duplicate and
provider locks still prevent a new initial draft. Unsupported copy still fails its
content gate. A decision maker, dramatic pain or buying trigger is optional.

The composer chooses a current verified Tier A gap, otherwise a verified Tier B
structural opportunity, otherwise a grounded service/information-to-enquiry proposal.
Verified platform presence supports a positive proposal; it does not establish that
the business lacks a website or suffers harm. An independently verified owned site
prevents a generic platform-presence fallback from implying an unresolved gap.

Stale activity can supply bounded, verified descriptive context without being called
recent or having its date renewed. AU compliance review, missing messaging verification
and an unresolved route remain send blockers. An ACCESS_BLOCKED domain is never a
positive domain finding; independent Tier B evidence may support different copy.

| Operator state | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| DRAFT_READY | Valid grounded draft with no remaining send blockers. |
| DRAFT_READY_SEND_BLOCKED | Valid grounded draft exists; approval remains gated. |
| NEEDS_COPY | Sufficient evidence, but composition/content validation failed. |
| NEEDS_RESEARCH | Identity, meaningful evidence or a truthful opportunity is insufficient. |
| PROTECTED | History, suppression, duplicate or provider state prevents another initial draft. |

The panel displays exact copy followed by separate send blockers. Operators may save
validated edits while sending is blocked. Current source evidence and source hashes,
route/timezone, contact locks, permission basis, AU review, actual messaging verification,
exact-content approval and provider claims remain authoritative before approval or Zoho
handoff. Saving an edit records no approval. MARK_SENT still requires its existing
explicit manual-send confirmation and approved/provider state.

The 2026-10-08 held audit used existing research only: 337 held records, 127 without
source-level protections, 210 source-protected records. An existing Zoho provider draft
adds one protected record. The remaining outcomes are 12 blocked but reviewable drafts,
114 genuine research holds, zero unblocked drafts and zero copy failures. Eight usable
drafts are additional: six new queue records and two recovered empty holds. Three other
unreviewed drafts were regenerated under the separated states. The operator-owned draft
and existing provider receipt were preserved exactly.

The one-off backfill compared all 409 research row hashes and all four review table
baselines before writing. It reused the existing exact-content native writer and
verified each result before continuing. Only eleven unreviewed draft rows were written;
source tabs, Arc Research, protected queue records and protection locks were unchanged.
Inbox/link writes are projections, not outreach approvals or source writes.

Local tests and eleven pure Cloud regressions passed. Native recovery, source/ledger
reconciliation and panel rendering were verified separately. Only the already-published
panel receives its new version; drafting, review, sync and Zoho workflows stay inactive
and manual. No research providers, approvals, sends, Zoho drafts or MARK_SENT ran.
